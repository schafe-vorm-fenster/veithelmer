If his alarm clock works, Anton gets up early in the morning to watch a woman walking past his window on her way to work. When a phone booth is installed in front of his house, he calls and, on behalf of the postal service, gives her a trip to the Harz Mountains for two. But on the day of the trip, his alarm clock fails him.
